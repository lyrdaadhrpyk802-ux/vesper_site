03 — Live sky

Earth, Moon & Sun

The Sun and Moon are shown where they really are right now. Distances are compressed so all three fit on one screen. Drop your own pin to be seen by visitors.

Live · real positions
Calculating the sky...

How to read this

Sun: the point on Earth directly under it right now.
Moon: its real direction. The percent is how much of it is lit.
Dim trail: where Sun and Moon were over the last 24 hours.
Bright trail: where they will be over the next 24 hours.
Pins: people who placed themselves on the planet.
Arc: two pins that are less than 1,500 km apart.
Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Click a pin to read it.
