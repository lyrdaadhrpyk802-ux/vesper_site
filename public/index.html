Place yourself

Click the planet, use your device location, or type your coordinates. Your name, a short message and a rounded location (about 10 km) become visible to every visitor.

How to get your coordinates:
On a computer: open Google Maps, right-click your exact spot, and the first line of the menu shows two numbers. Click them to copy.
On a phone: open Google Maps, press and hold your spot, and the coordinates appear at the bottom of the screen. Tap to copy.
Paste them here in the form latitude, longitude, with the latitude first. Negative numbers are for the south and west.
